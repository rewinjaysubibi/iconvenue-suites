# Logo Setup Instructions

## Save Your Logo

Please save your Icon Venue & Suites logo image as:
- **Filename**: `logo.png`
- **Location**: `public/images/logo.png`

## Logo Specifications

- **Recommended size**: 512x512 pixels or larger (will be automatically scaled)
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background (recommended) or JPG
- **Aspect ratio**: Square or the original logo aspect ratio

## Where the Logo Appears

The logo has been integrated into the following locations:

1. **Login Page** - Displayed at the top of the staff login form
2. **Admin Sidebar** - Shows in the sidebar header for all admin/staff pages
3. **Public Navigation** - Appears in the top navigation bar on all public pages
4. **Home Page Hero** - Featured prominently on the homepage hero section

## After Saving the Logo

Once you save the logo file, refresh your browser to see it appear throughout the system.

If the logo doesn't appear:
1. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Make sure the file is named exactly `logo.png`
3. Verify the file is in the correct location: `public/images/logo.png`
